# Timeout policy

- xdispd activation deadline: 10 seconds by default.
- xdispd graceful stop deadline: 3 seconds.
- Escalation: SIGTERM immediately, SIGKILL only when the 3-second stop source
  fires and `ChildPid` is still nonzero.
- Stop duration is measured with `std::chrono::steady_clock`.
- The child status milestones carry a monotonic millisecond timestamp; journal
  lines provide supporting wall-clock timestamps.
- No xdispd, USB, GUD, or Mir timeout was changed for T05.
