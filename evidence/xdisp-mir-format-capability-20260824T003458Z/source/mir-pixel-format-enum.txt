The deployed Android2 allocator advertises only 1, 2, 6, and 7 at runtime.
The exact runtime line was:
available_mir_formats=1,2,6,7 available_mir_format_names=abgr8888,xbgr8888,rgb888,rgb565

Compatibility note: the local Android2 format-conversion header also references
mir_pixel_format_rgba_10101002, but that symbol is not present in the deployed
Mir common.h enum listing used for this audit and was not runtime-advertised.
It is therefore treated as an unresolved Android compatibility reference, not
as a verified MirPixelFormat enum/value in this capability matrix.
