E3-B02 post-A/B final candidate restoration
============================================

All actions here occurred after qualification and are excluded from counted
cycles.

After the controlled new-module A/B, the final module remained loaded but the
diagnostic helper was intentionally inactive. Reconnect initially left the Pi
UDC `not attached`; phone xHCI enumerated the hub and other downstream devices
but timed out reading the Pi gadget descriptor on port 1-1.3.

The Pi receiver's last telemetry proved `aggregate_state=Idle`,
`aio_state=Idle`, `currently_owned=false`, `processing_failed=0`, and
`unsafe_teardown_suppressed=0`. The Pi gadget service was therefore safely
restarted once as excluded lab restoration. It returned active as PID 5623,
NRestarts=0, with UDC state `configured`. The phone then enumerated GUD as
1-1.3 `1d50:614d` at 480 Mbit/s and recreated `/dev/dri/card1` without another
phone role write.

The final candidate helper was enabled and started as PID 5756, NRestarts=0.
The retained A/B ActivationRequested flag had automatically launched a child
when card1 returned, so normal `org.lomiri.XDisp1.Deactivate` was called once.
