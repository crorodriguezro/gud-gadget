Presentation-rate observation during E3-B02
===========================================

Classification: separate transport/performance follow-up; no E3-B02 source
change touched the connected frame path.

Before the counted active detach, the operator reported fewer visible frames
than the prior day's tests. The managed child's final report objectively showed:

  received_fps=61
  submitted_fps=61
  presented_fps=4
  dropped_fps=56
  drop_percent=92.0149
  submit_us_avg=201101
  submit_us_p95=1000000

In the later post-qualification trackpad diagnostic, the fresh child reported
15 presented FPS over 12.32 seconds, with 61 received/submitted FPS and 73.77%
drops. This confirms variability and a real presentation bottleneck, but not a
causal link to the root-port recovery monitor. The monitor is armed only after
GUD disconnect and is cancelled on normal GUD probe; it does no frame-path work
while the display is connected.
