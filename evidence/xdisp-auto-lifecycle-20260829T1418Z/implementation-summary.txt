The selected production design is one resident xdispd systemd service. It arms
a libudev DRM monitor, performs one bounded startup scan, and owns one managed
mirgud child only while an exact live GUD DRM instance is usable.

Discovery selects primary cardN nodes by DRM driver identity (gud), connected
1280x720 capability, udev sysfs identity, and device number. Before spawn the
node is reopened and both device number and sysfs identity are revalidated.
Card numbering is diagnostic only.

Removal invalidates the exact instance, stops the child, releases presenter/KMS
and Mir resources, and applies bounded containment for the known Mir release
stall. Re-add creates a fresh fd and child. Duplicate events are idempotent.
The resident service remains idle without GUD. xdisp-status exposes presence,
identity, bridge/child/output/presenter state, lifecycle state, and the last
event. D-Bus Enable/Disable/Activate/Deactivate and poison controls remain
available; automatic activation is enabled by default.
