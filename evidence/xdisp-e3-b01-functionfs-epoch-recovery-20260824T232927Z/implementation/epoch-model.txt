BulkReceiveState and ExactAioState now include AbortedBySessionDestruction.
Both owners track a monotonically increasing USB epoch ID. ENABLE starts an
epoch only from Idle or AbortedBySessionDestruction. A boundary seen while an
AIO is owned poisons/contains that old transaction until its completion is
harvested. Retirement clears current ownership metadata but preserves the last
retired epoch and transaction sequence as historical disposition.
