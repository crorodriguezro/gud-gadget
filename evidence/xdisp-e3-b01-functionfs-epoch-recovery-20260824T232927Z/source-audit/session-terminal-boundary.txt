Safe retirement rule
====================

An old USB epoch may be retired only after:

1. FUNCTIONFS_DISABLE, FUNCTIONFS_UNBIND, or a subsequent FUNCTIONFS_BIND/ENABLE
   has proven that FunctionFS synchronously destroyed or disabled the prior
   endpoint set; AND
2. the old logical transaction's sole accepted Linux-AIO completion has been
   harvested; AND
3. the endpoint AIO queue is proven empty.

The historical disposition is ABORTED_BY_SESSION_DESTRUCTION. This is never an
Idle or successful transition inside the old epoch. A new ENABLE may then start
a strictly increasing epoch in Idle. SUSPEND and RESUME preserve the same epoch
and ownership.
